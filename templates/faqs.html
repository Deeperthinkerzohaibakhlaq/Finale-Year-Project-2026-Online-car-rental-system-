Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting a car with AutoHire — from booking to drop-off.

Reservations & Payments
How much does it cost to rent a car from AutoHire?
AutoHire daily rates start from 5 EUR per day for economy cars in Cyprus, with rates varying by location, vehicle category, and season. All rates include unlimited mileage, basic CDW insurance, and free shuttle service from major airports (Larnaca & Paphos). There are no hidden booking fees. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay. Premium vehicles, SUVs, and automatic cars are available at higher rate tiers.
How can I book a car with AutoHire?
The best way is to book directly on our website (www.autohire.com). You may also find AutoHire on comparison sites, but direct booking ensures you get our full customer support and transparent terms. Our reservation team is also available via phone or email to assist you.
What documents do I need to rent a car?
You need: (1) A valid driving license held for at least one year (2) A passport or national ID card (3) A credit card in the main driver's name for the security deposit. International visitors from outside the EU need an International Driving Permit (IDP) in addition to their national license. All documents must be originals.
Can I pay in cash?
No, cash payments are not accepted. We accept Visa and Mastercard credit cards for both payment and security deposit. Apple Pay is available for online bookings.
Is prepayment mandatory when booking on AutoHire's website?
No, prepayment is optional. At checkout you can choose "pay in advance" (discount applies) or "pay on arrival". We do not prioritize prepaid reservations, so all bookings are secure.
What are the age requirements to rent from AutoHire?
Drivers must be at least 23 years old and hold a license for 12+ months. Young drivers (21–22) are accepted with a daily fee of €10. Inexperienced driver or very young (18–20) may require a one-time fee. Senior drivers (70+) also have a small daily surcharge. Contact our support for exact details.
I need to extend my rental. What should I do?
Log into "Manage Booking" on our website, enter your email and reservation number, then modify the drop-off date. The extra cost is shown and must be paid online. Extensions are confirmed only after payment.
Can I get the exact same car model as shown on the website?
We guarantee the car category (similar size/specifications). If the exact model is unavailable, we offer a free upgrade to a higher class. You can express preferences and we'll do our best to accommodate.
What is your fuel policy?
You receive the car with a full tank (or level indicated). Please return it with the same amount of fuel. If not, you'll be charged a refueling fee (€20 + fuel cost). Rental price does not include fuel.
Customer Service & Shuttle
How does the airport shuttle work?
AutoHire provides a free shuttle service at Larnaca and Paphos airports. After collecting luggage, call the number in your booking confirmation. We’ll pick you up within 10 minutes and drive you directly to our rental office (approx 10–12 min). Shuttle runs 24/7.
How can I contact the Booking or Customer Care department?
Booking Department: +357 123 4567 (Mon-Fri 8:30–22:00, Sat–Sun 9:00–18:00) or email bookings@autohire.com. Customer Care: customercare@autohire.com for complaints, lost items, or post-rental issues.
My flight is delayed. What should I do?
Simply inform our Customer Care about your new arrival time. We’ll hold your car and adjust pick-up accordingly – no extra charge within reasonable delay. Call the airport shuttle number.
I returned the car earlier / with more fuel. Can I get a refund?
For early returns (at least 24h before scheduled drop-off), we issue a discount voucher equivalent to unused days (max 3 days). For extra fuel, you receive a voucher as well. Vouchers have no expiry and are valid at any AutoHire location.
What happens if I return the car late?
A grace period of 1 hour is provided. After that, you’ll be charged an additional rental day. If you anticipate delay, please contact the office to extend your rental.
Locations & Fleet
Where does AutoHire operate?
AutoHire operates across Cyprus with major locations in Larnaca, Limassol, Nicosia, and Paphos. All airport locations offer free shuttle. We’re also expanding to select cities in Europe soon.
Can I pick up in one city and drop off in another?
Yes, one-way rentals are available between our Cyprus offices. Extra fees apply depending on distance. Contact our reservation team for a quote.
What are your operating hours?
Airport locations: 24 hours / 7 days, including holidays (shuttle 24/7). City offices: 8:00 – 20:00 daily. Customer support available 24/7 for emergencies.
Do you have automatic cars?
Yes, we offer a wide range of automatic vehicles across all categories. Booking in advance is recommended, especially during high season.
Is unlimited mileage included?
All short-term rentals include unlimited mileage at no extra cost. Long-term subscriptions may have mileage packages – see our car subscription page for details.
What should I do in case of an accident or breakdown?
Immediately call our 24/7 emergency line (written on your keychain). For accidents: ensure safety, call police if needed, take photos, exchange details. For breakdown: we’ll arrange roadside assistance and a replacement vehicle if necessary.
Deposits & Insurance
The deposit was released but I haven't received the money back. What now?
We release the pre-authorization immediately upon return. Your bank may take 2–3 weeks to make the funds available again. If you received a cancellation receipt, the hold is lifted; please check with your bank.
I lost the car keys. What do I do?
Contact our Customer Care immediately. We’ll arrange a key replacement (fee applies). Do not attempt to start the car with damaged keys.
Do I need to wash the car before returning?
If you opted for the deposit option (full responsibility), yes – we require the car clean to inspect for damages. Otherwise, a cleaning fee may apply. Returning it clean helps ensure faster deposit release.
My deposit is blocked due to damages. How long will it take?
Our damage department will contact you within 20 days. We work with your insurance provider if applicable. Cases are handled chronologically. Please be patient and provide any third-party insurance documents.
Can I smoke in the car?
No, smoking in our vehicles is strictly prohibited. A €124 penalty fee applies if smoke odor or burns are detected.
Invoices & Lost Items
How can I get an invoice for my rental?
At pick-up you can specify invoice details (personal or company). We'll email the invoice within one business day. Contact Customer Care if not received.
I need to cancel my prepaid booking. When will I get a refund?
Cancel more than 48h before pick-up: refund minus €30 admin fee within 5-7 business days. Cancel less than 48h: amount is non-refundable but you receive a voucher for additional services. Use the "Cancel" button in your confirmation email.
I left personal belongings in the car. What should I do?
Email Customer Care with a description, rental contract number, and dates. If found, you can collect from the office, send a friend, or arrange courier shipment (fees may apply).
