Our journey — where mobility meets trust

From a small local fleet to a full-scale digital rental platform, AutoHire keeps moving Cyprus forward.

Founded by travelers, for travelers.

AutoHire was created by a team of passionate drivers and tech enthusiasts who recognized that traditional car rental lacked flexibility, clarity, and fairness. We set out to build an online platform where booking a car is as easy as ordering a ride — with honest rates, minute-level pricing, and the freedom to cancel or adjust.

Since our launch in 2021, we have served thousands of customers across Cyprus — from tourists exploring Paphos to professionals needing reliable monthly wheels. Our legacy continues to grow, powered by a dedicated local support team and a constantly expanding fleet.
